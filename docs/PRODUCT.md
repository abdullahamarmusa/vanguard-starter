# Product overview

VANGUARD is an independent, multi-tenant business intelligence and revenue
operations platform for small software development agencies and independent
software studios. Its initial hypothesis is that these teams need a disciplined
way to connect what they sell with whom they should sell it to.

## Vision

Over time, VANGUARD may support business intelligence, offer and portfolio
analysis, ideal customer profile generation, authorized market research,
company and contact discovery, evidence-backed qualification, outreach
preparation, campaign operations, reply intelligence, CRM, financial
performance, and strategy support.

## MVP: Offer-to-Lead Intelligence

The first workflow is deliberately narrow:

1. Define an offer, its outcomes, audience, and commercial constraints.
2. Describe and review the ideal customer profile derived from that offer.
3. Use an authorized, licensed source to gather company evidence.
4. Qualify companies with structured, validated recommendations tied to
   evidence and captured source metadata.
5. Let a person review qualification and prepare outreach drafts.

The MVP does **not** send campaigns, scrape websites without authorization,
claim that a provider is integrated before it is, or allow an AI model to make
authorization, database, or send decisions.

## Validation questions

- Do small agencies have a repeatable, costly offer-to-prospect workflow?
- Which evidence is useful enough to justify a score and a human review?
- Which authorized data sources cover the target customers and permitted use?
- What roles, audit history, data retention, and CRM boundaries do buyers need?
- Can customers judge lead quality from cited evidence rather than opaque scores?

Pricing, legal terms, target jurisdictions, and any provider-specific claims
remain open decisions and must be validated before launch.
